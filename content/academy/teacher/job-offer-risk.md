# Higher pay, higher uncertainty

## Objectives and activity

Learners compare expected income, probability, reserve runway, career learning, and downside capacity. Use the job-offer Decision Lab case. Teams must propose a strong option, a reasonable alternative, and a condition that would change the recommendation.

## Prompts, misconceptions, and rubric

Ask how many months of essential costs remain, which risks can be negotiated, and which benefits are not captured by salary. Correct “higher expected value is always better” and “uncertainty means never act.” Score quantitative comparison, downside plan, non-financial factors, alternatives, and revision trigger from 0–3. Extension: draft five due-diligence questions for the employer.
