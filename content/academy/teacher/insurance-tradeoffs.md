# Higher deductible or higher premium?

## Objectives and activity

Learners connect deductible, premium, exclusions, maximum loss, probability, and reserve. Teams compare the insurance case for two households with different liquid savings. For 90 minutes, add one correlated risk and a policy exclusion.

## Prompts, misconceptions, and rubric

Ask which loss is affordable today, what remains uninsured, and when self-insurance is reasonable. Correct “insurance prevents loss” and “lowest premium is best.” Score cover reading, loss capacity, probability calibration, reserve impact, and justification from 0–3. Extension: write three questions for an insurer before purchase.
