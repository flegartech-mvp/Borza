# Scam-signal triage

## Objectives

Learners identify evidence-based warning signals, avoid accusing legitimate uncertainty, and choose a safe independent verification path.

## Preparation and activity

Choose three Scam Detector scenarios of increasing difficulty. Learners first mark signals alone, then compare evidence in pairs. Require every group to identify one suspicious feature, one neutral feature, and one safe action. For 90 minutes, groups rewrite one scenario into a fair, transparent offer.

## Discussion prompts

- Is this feature proof of fraud, a warning signal, or neutral?
- Which source is independent of the sender?
- What should you do if credentials or money were already shared?
- How can urgency change decision quality?

## Misconceptions and rubric

Correct “all crypto is fraud,” “professional design proves legitimacy,” and “knowing my name proves identity.” Score 0–3 for signal classification, rationale, verification, immediate safety, and calibration. Extension: build a verification checklist for a fictional provider.
