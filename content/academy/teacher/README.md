# Borza Academy teacher materials

These original materials support anonymous, discussion-first classroom sessions. They are not graded financial advice. Use fictional or aggregate examples and never ask learners to disclose income, debt, account balances, or personal financial distress.

## Shared 45-minute pattern

1. Frame the decision and safety rule (5 min).
2. Individual anonymous response (5 min).
3. Small-group evidence and calculation (12 min).
4. Whole-class comparison of reasoning (15 min).
5. Exit ticket: one assumption, one risk, one next action (8 min).

For 90 minutes, add the extension calculation, second scenario, peer critique, and reflection. Assess process quality separately from the chosen outcome.

## Privacy and inclusion

- Classroom display names are pseudonyms; codes expire and responses are aggregated.
- Do not collect email addresses, real names, financial account data, or free-form personal financial stories.
- Offer a paper or verbal alternative. Do not rank learners by wealth, returns, speed, or risk appetite.
- If a learner discloses fraud, coercion, or hardship, stop the simulation and follow the institution's safeguarding procedure.
