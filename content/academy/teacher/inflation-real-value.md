# Nominal or real?

## Objectives and activity

Learners distinguish nominal from real value and explain compounding without implying certainty. Compare €1,000 held for five years under two illustrative inflation and return assumptions. Show the formula and label every assumption.

## Prompts, misconceptions, and rubric

Ask why a positive nominal return can still lose purchasing power, why fees matter, and why a single forecast is insufficient. Correct “past rate equals future rate” and “cash is risk-free in every sense.” Score formula use, assumption labels, real-value interpretation, uncertainty, and communication from 0–3. Extension: sensitivity table for three inflation rates.
