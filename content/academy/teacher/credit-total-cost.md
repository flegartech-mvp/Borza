# Credit: payment or total cost?

## Objectives and preparation

Learners compare amount financed, deposit, term, fees, total payable, and effect on emergency liquidity. Prepare the car and leasing cases plus a calculator.

## Activity

Teams calculate total cash outflow and list missing information before voting. In 45 minutes compare two offers; in 90 minutes add an income-loss stress test and contract-exit scenario.

## Prompts, misconceptions, and rubric

Ask which variable drives total cost, whether the payment remains affordable after a shock, and which term requires verification. Correct “lower monthly payment is cheaper” and “approval means affordability.” Score calculation, completeness, liquidity, contract risk, and explanation from 0–3. Extension: design a transparent comparison table.
