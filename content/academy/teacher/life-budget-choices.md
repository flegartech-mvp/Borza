# Life budget under pressure

## Objectives

Learners distinguish fixed, variable, and irregular costs; preserve liquidity; and explain trade-offs without treating the highest ending balance as the only success measure.

## Preparation

Open the Life Simulator, choose two contrasting profiles, and display the assumption note. Emphasise that all people and amounts are fictional learning examples.

## Activity

In 45 minutes, run housing, transport, emergency, and saving rounds. Before each vote, teams name the constraint, calculation, uncertainty, and reversible next step. For 90 minutes, complete all rounds and compare how the same choice affects two profiles differently.

## Discussion prompts

- Which cost looks small monthly but large annually?
- When does liquidity matter more than a higher expected return?
- Which decision changes if income is unstable?
- What evidence would make you revise your choice?

## Misconceptions and rubric

Correct “cheap equals lowest monthly payment” and “a good outcome proves a good decision.” Score 0–3 each for calculation, assumptions, risk identification, alternatives, and reflection. Extension: design a profile with a balanced but genuinely difficult trade-off.
