# Practical finance content standard

Canonical practical collections are Life Simulator, scam scenarios, decision cases, competences, and classroom activities under `content/academy/`. The validator requires stable kebab-case IDs, content versions, exact DE/SL/EN coverage, valid competence references, safe internal actions, numerical Life effects, contrasting decision quality, calibrated scam signals, and existing teacher materials.

Every case must disclose known data, identify missing information, require a calculation where useful, include at least one strong and one weak/dangerous process, explain feedback, ask for reflection, and link to a contextual next action. Brands and people are fictional. Scam content must distinguish warning signals from neutral evidence and provide independent verification steps.

Facts that can drift require a dated primary source. Scenario assumptions must be labelled as illustrative. Original instruction is mandatory; do not scrape or imply regulator, university, bank, or school affiliation.
