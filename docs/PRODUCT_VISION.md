# Borza Academy Product Vision

## Practical finance platform

The Academy now extends its core learning loop into everyday decisions: a Slovenia-focused Life Simulator, Decision Lab, calibrated Scam Detector, anonymous Teacher Mode, evidence-backed Competence Passport, and controlled Socratic Mentor. These are one connected evidence system, not disconnected engagement widgets. Every practical attempt should lead to feedback, reflection, a competence update, and a contextual next action.

## Promise

**Learn finance. Practise trading. Build real market skills.**

Borza Academy is a practical financial decision-making and risk-management academy for European beginners, students, schools, and aspiring traders. It exists so learners can make and review consequential decisions in a protected environment before real money makes an error expensive.

## Product position

Borza combines a premium course, a modern learning application, an educational simulator, and a professional decision toolkit. It is not a news reader, course marketplace, signal service, brokerage, crypto casino, or trading-guru funnel.

The primary launch areas are:

1. Financial foundations.
2. Risk Management, the flagship reference path.
3. Trading foundations.
4. Practical personal finance, introduced through the school programme and expanded as authored content becomes review-ready.
5. Decision-making through deterministic simulation.

Planned paths remain visibly secondary until their lessons, exercises, review evidence, and sources meet the learning standard.

## Core learning loop

> Learn a concept → apply it to a realistic case → use a calculator or chart → make a simulated decision → receive process feedback → reflect in a journal.

Every surface should move a learner through that loop. Lessons, quizzes, glossary, review cards, calculators, charts, simulation, and journal are evidence types in one system—not separate mini-applications.

## Audiences

- Beginners who want durable personal-finance and market foundations.
- Secondary-school, vocational, technical, economics, and business students.
- Teachers who need practical cases and reviewable assessment.
- Aspiring traders who need risk discipline before tactics.
- Supporters who value measurable, responsible financial literacy.

## Success

Borza succeeds when a learner can explain the reasoning, constraints, uncertainties, and alternatives behind a financial decision. Simulated profit is never sufficient evidence. A controlled losing decision can demonstrate stronger learning than a reckless win.

## Boundaries

- Education, not personal financial advice.
- No real orders, brokerage integration, signals, copy trading, or profit promises.
- No fabricated returns, testimonials, adoption, affiliations, or supporter claims.
- Simulation and historical-style data are always labelled.
- Young and inexperienced learners receive risk, fraud, privacy, and consumer-protection context before complex products.
- German, Slovenian, and English remain first-class languages; German is the default.
