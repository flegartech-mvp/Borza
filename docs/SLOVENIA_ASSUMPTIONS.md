Rent, food, transport, insurance, health costs, net incomes, credit offers, inflation paths, and investment effects are rounded fictional assumptions selected to create trade-offs. They are not market averages, quotes, eligibility results, or forecasts. Review dated facts before every release and increment the scenario version when a factual or numerical change alters learner decisions.
