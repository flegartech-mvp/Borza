# Product Roadmap

## Now — reference-quality core

- Keep Risk Management the flagship path.
- Complete expert and language review of the eight risk lessons and their exercises.
- Make simulator precommitment, process feedback, lesson review, and journal reflection one flow.
- Maintain clear public positioning, schools, and impact pages.
- Pilot the Life Simulator, Decision Lab, Scam Detector, Teacher Mode, and Competence Passport with explicit evidence and privacy boundaries.
- Keep planned paths secondary until they meet the learning standard.

Exit evidence: content validation, direct simulator/calculator tests, frontend checks and build, backend tests and migrations, browser/accessibility review.

## Next — school pilot readiness

- Teacher review of the Slovenian programme.
- Review and refine the six printable teacher activities generated from maintainable sources.
- Pilot protocol, consent/privacy materials, minimal data plan, and accessibility checklist.
- Pre/post scenario tasks and a teacher workload measure.
- Verified organisational contact channel.

Exit evidence: signed-off pilot protocol and named accountable operator. This does not imply public-authority approval.

## Later — validated expansion

- Expand Practical Finance Skills with payslips, tax examples, insurance, credit, fraud, and consumer protection.
- Complete Investing only after reviewable long-term, diversified, fee-aware content exists.
- Expand additional paths one at a time based on demonstrated learner need.
- Consider public-benefit or open educational-resource releases where licensing, governance, and maintenance are sustainable.
- Add payment functionality only after the legal and reporting boundary is complete.

## Explicit non-goals

- Live news or market-data dependency.
- Brokerage or real-order integration.
- Signals, copy trading, profit rankings, or leverage rewards.
- Scraped courses, institutional branding, or implied certification.
- Workers, schedulers, Valkey, or microservices for ordinary learning state.
