# Competence Passport

The Passport groups versioned evidence across budgeting, saving, emergency planning, interest/inflation, credit/debt, consumer protection, insurance, investing foundations, risk management, scam awareness, digital safety, decision-making, and reflection discipline.

Levels are transparent heuristics over recent server-scored evidence: not started, introduced, practising, demonstrated, and strong. Each card shows evidence count, recent source type/version/date, and a contextual next action. Demo evidence is explicitly browser-local. The print view can be saved as PDF.

The Passport is not a diploma, regulator-approved certificate, professional qualification, credit assessment, investment record, or evidence of future returns. A single attempt cannot create strong mastery, and missing evidence is shown as unavailable rather than fabricated.
