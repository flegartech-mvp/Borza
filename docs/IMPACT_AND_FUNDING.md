# Impact and Funding

## Problem

Financial knowledge is often abstract, fragmented, or mixed with product promotion and confident outcome stories. Young and inexperienced learners need a protected environment where they can calculate, explain, simulate, receive process feedback, and reflect.

## Beneficiaries

- European beginners and students.
- Slovenian secondary, vocational, technical, economics, and business classrooms.
- Teachers seeking practical, multilingual material.
- Aspiring traders who need risk discipline without a brokerage connection.
- Families and communities that benefit from safer financial decisions.

## Current capabilities

Borza currently includes structured multilingual paths, authored lessons and quizzes, glossary and spaced review, calculator and chart exercises, deterministic simulation, a Slovenian Life Simulator, Decision Lab, Scam Detector, Teacher Mode, Competence Passport, controlled Socratic Mentor, a journal, learner progress, optional Supabase authentication, and a proposed Slovenian school programme.

These are product capabilities, not evidence of adoption or impact. There are no public usage totals, testimonials, school logos, supporters, or pilot outcomes unless verified evidence is added later.

## Measurement model

A pilot should predefine:

- completion and retention;
- change in scenario-based knowledge before and after instruction;
- calculation accuracy;
- ability to identify scams and unsafe financial behaviour;
- quality of written reasoning and risk limits;
- accessibility and teacher workload;
- adverse or confusing experiences;
- limitations, missing data, and attrition.

## Roadmap

1. Independent subject review of the Risk Management path.
2. Teacher review of the 35-hour school proposal.
3. One-class pilot with agreed pre/post evidence.
4. Accessibility and privacy review.
5. Transparent pilot report, including limitations.
6. Repeatable materials and facilitator training for additional schools.

## Ways to support

- Individual support after a lawful payment structure exists.
- School sponsorships.
- Foundation grants.
- Educational partnerships.
- Corporate social-responsibility funding.
- Responsible angel investment with education-first governance.
- Subject-matter and teaching contributors.
- Pilot schools and teachers.

| Area | Example share | Evidence expected |

These percentages are planning examples, not a funding commitment.

## Payment boundary

Proposed classroom-support levels are €250, €500, €1,500, and €5,000. They are planning anchors, not products, invoices, donation commitments, or investment terms.

No donation or investment checkout is enabled. A consent-based form can store organisation, role, email, interest type, support level, and message for a configurable retention period (180 days by default). If the API is unavailable, the interface explicitly says the brief was copied locally and not submitted. Payment remains blocked until the legal recipient, governance, tax, privacy, refund, safeguarding, and reporting duties are defined.
